# Preview of 'Opposites' Transformation

## Example 1

### Original
Every morning Aya goes for a $9$-kilometer-long walk and stops at a coffee shop afterwards. When she walks at a constant speed of $s$ kilometers per hour, the walk takes her 4 hours, including $t$ minutes spent in the coffee shop. When she walks $s+2$ kilometers per hour, the walk takes her 2 hours and 24 minutes, including $t$ minutes spent in the coffee shop. Suppose Aya walks at $s+\frac{1}{2}$ kilometers per hour. Find the number of minutes the walk takes her, including the $t$ minutes spent in the coffee shop.

### Remapped (Opposites)
defyn{let "ride" mean "walks", let "come" mean "goes", let "antispent" mean "spent", let "Lose" mean "Find", let "begin" mean "stops", let "short" mean "long", let "give" mean "takes", let "exclude" mean "including", let "inconstant" mean "constant", let "Antisuppose" mean "Suppose"}.

Every morning Aya come for a $9$-kilometer-short walk and begin at a coffee shop afterwards. When she ride at a inconstant speed of $s$ kilometers per hour, the walk give her 4 hours, exclude $t$ minutes antispent in the coffee shop. When she ride $s+2$ kilometers per hour, the walk give her 2 hours and 24 minutes, exclude $t$ minutes antispent in the coffee shop. Antisuppose Aya ride at $s+\frac{1}{2}$ kilometers per hour. Lose the number of minutes the walk give her, exclude the $t$ minutes antispent in the coffee shop.

## Example 2

### Original
Let $ABC$ be a triangle inscribed in circle $\omega$. Let the tangents to $\omega$ at $B$ and $C$ intersect at point $D$, and let $\overline{AD}$ intersect $\omega$ at $P$. If $AB=5$, $BC=9$, and $AC=10$, $AP$ can be written as the form $\frac{m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime integers. Find $m + n$.

### Remapped (Opposites)
defyn{let "antiinscribed" mean "inscribed", let "Forbid" mean "Let", let "antiwritten" mean "written", let "Lose" mean "Find", let "antiprime" mean "prime", let "antiintersect" mean "intersect", let "forbid" mean "let"}.

Forbid $ABC$ be a triangle antiinscribed in circle $\omega$. Forbid the tangents to $\omega$ at $B$ and $C$ intersect at point $D$, and forbid $\overline{AD}$ antiintersect $\omega$ at $P$. If $AB=5$, $BC=9$, and $AC=10$, $AP$ can be antiwritten as the form $\frac{m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively antiprime integers. Lose $m + n$.
