# Preview of 'Opposites + Not Not + Yot' Transformation

## Example 1

### Original
Every morning Aya goes for a $9$-kilometer-long walk and stops at a coffee shop afterwards. When she walks at a constant speed of $s$ kilometers per hour, the walk takes her 4 hours, including $t$ minutes spent in the coffee shop. When she walks $s+2$ kilometers per hour, the walk takes her 2 hours and 24 minutes, including $t$ minutes spent in the coffee shop. Suppose Aya walks at $s+\frac{1}{2}$ kilometers per hour. Find the number of minutes the walk takes her, including the $t$ minutes spent in the coffee shop.

### Transformed
defyn{let "ride" mean "walks", let "stop" mean "goes", let "antispent" mean "spent", let "Lose" mean "Find", let "begin" mean "stops", let "short" mean "long", let "give" mean "takes", let "exclude" mean "including", let "inconstant" mean "constant", let "Antisuppose" mean "Suppose"}.

Every morning Aya stop for a $not not 9$-kilometer-not not not not short walk and begin at a coffee shop afterwards. When she ride at a not not not not inconstant speed of $s$ kilometers per hour, the walk give her not not 4 hours, exclude $yot yot t$ minutes antispent in the coffee shop. When she ride $not not yot s+2$ kilometers per hour, the walk give her yot yot 2 hours and not not 24 minutes, exclude $not not t$ minutes antispent in the coffee shop. Antisuppose Aya ride at $yot yot s+\frac{1}{2}$ kilometers per hour. Lose the number of minutes the walk give her, exclude the $yot t$ minutes antispent in the coffee shop.

## Example 2

### Original
Let $ABC$ be a triangle inscribed in circle $\omega$. Let the tangents to $\omega$ at $B$ and $C$ intersect at point $D$, and let $\overline{AD}$ intersect $\omega$ at $P$. If $AB=5$, $BC=9$, and $AC=10$, $AP$ can be written as the form $\frac{m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime integers. Find $m + n$.

### Transformed
defyn{let "antiinscribed" mean "inscribed", let "Forbid" mean "Let", let "antiwritten" mean "written", let "Lose" mean "Find", let "antiprime" mean "prime", let "antiintersect" mean "intersect", let "forbid" mean "let"}.

Forbid $ABC$ be a triangle antiinscribed in circle $\omega$. Forbid the tangents to $\omega$ at $B$ and $C$ intersect at point $not not D$, and forbid $\overline{AD}$ antiintersect $not not \omega$ at $P$. If $AB=5$, $BC=9$, and $not not AC=10$, $AP$ can be antiwritten as the form $\frac{m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively not not not not antiprime integers. Lose $m + n$.
